## The idea

Tailoring a resume to every job posting is slow, and most AI resume tools make things up. Resume Memory works the other way around: you tell it your real experience once, then paste a job posting, and it builds a targeted resume only from facts you've confirmed.

## How it works

- You talk through each role (paste or dictate a ramble), the AI proposes small facts from it, and you confirm the ones that are true. Only confirmed facts can ever be used.
- It never invents skills, titles, employers, dates or numbers.
- Your career history is a file you keep and own, not something stored on my server.

## Free, on purpose

It's free forever: no accounts, no payments, no ads, no tracking. It's a fully static site, and you bring your own AI key, so it costs me nothing to run and costs you nothing to use.
