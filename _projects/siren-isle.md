## Project overview

During the RPG Maker Horror Game Jam #4 in 2024, a team of four of my friends and I worked on Siren Isle. I created all of the music and sound effects for this game and worked collaboratively on writing the dialogue and story. The story is based largely on *The Shadow over Innsmouth* by H. P. Lovecraft.

**We placed 9th overall in the competition.**
