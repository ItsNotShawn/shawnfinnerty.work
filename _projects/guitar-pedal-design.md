## Guitar effect LFO generator

The idea for this guitar pedal came after watching a breakdown of the recording and mixing process behind David Bowie's *Heroes*. In the video, they discussed how Brian Eno ran Robert Fripp's guitar through a synthesizer, producing a sound that became an integral part of the song and something completely unique to that recording.

{% include embed.html youtube="7Q2scPrc1WE" start="288" title="The breakdown of Heroes that started the idea" %}

I found the combined use of guitar and synthesizer incredibly inspiring, and it led me to think about a modern equivalent: what if a guitar could be routed through a modular synthesizer-style system?

For those unfamiliar, [modular synthesizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_synthesizer) work very differently from traditional synthesizers. Instead of having a fixed internal signal path, the different functions of the instrument are separated into individual modules that are connected externally using patch cables. This approach removes the fixed structure of traditional instruments and replaces it with a flexible system where the user can decide how signals flow and interact.

## Why modular?

Thinking about this raised two interesting questions. Why would someone use a large modular synthesizer when compact synthesizers already exist? And similarly, why would someone route a guitar through a synthesizer when guitar pedals already provide many effects?

The answer in both cases is the same: control. Modular systems allow the user to design their own signal paths and create behaviors that would be difficult or impossible with fixed devices.

A major part of that flexibility comes from modulating effects themselves. In modular synthesis, control signals can be routed to parameters so that settings change automatically over time. One explanation I found especially useful described this as adding "invisible hands" to a control, allowing values to move on their own without direct human input. That idea opens up an enormous range of sonic possibilities.

## The pedal

My goal was to make that level of experimentation accessible on a guitar pedalboard. I envisioned a pedal that could act as a central modulation source, allowing one LFO to influence multiple parameters across a pedalboard and create sounds that would normally require direct manual adjustment.
