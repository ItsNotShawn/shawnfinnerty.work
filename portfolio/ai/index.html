AI, on my terms

AI isn't going anywhere. So instead of pretending I don't use it, I'd rather be upfront about how I do, and point it at things I think should exist.

Where I stand

I use AI tools to make a lot of things. I think they work best when a person stays in charge: I decide what gets made, what it should feel like, and what's true. The tool helps me get there faster.

A lot of what's being built with AI is designed to lock people in or charge them for the basics. I'd rather make it work for me on my terms, and use it to build things that are free and accessible to everyone. That's where most of my AI projects are headed.
