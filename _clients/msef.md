  - title: "Module template"
    service: "Creative & Design"
    year: 2026
    summary: "A Canva template that MSEF can use to build a module on any topic: a clickable student deck and a matching teacher guide, in MSEF's brand."
    # image: /images/clients/msef/template-map.jpg
    details: |
      - **One clear path.** Every page is one step: the big idea, a clickable map of topic areas, a page per area, then idea building, narrowing down and planning.
      - **Works for any topic.** Map layouts for four or five areas, and notes on every page telling the next author what to write.
      - **Printable worksheets.** New "My Ideas" and "My Project Idea" sheets that go straight into a student's project notebook.
      - **A "Check Your Idea" checklist** (time, materials, safety, data) in place of a yes/no flowchart that was hard to follow.
      - **Easy to click through.** Back, Map and Next buttons on every page, so it works as an interactive deck, not just slides.
      - **A teacher guide** for each module, with what to prepare, a launch video, the areas at a glance and safety notes.
      - Went through several rounds of feedback with MSEF, including a brand pass that moved from bold solid colors to a lighter page with a colored header.
  - title: "Food and Motion modules"
    service: "Creative & Design"
    year: 2026
    summary: "Moved MSEF's original Food and Motion modules onto the new template, each with a student deck and a teacher guide."
    # image: /images/clients/msef/food-area.jpg
    details: |
      - **Food Systems:** four areas: Production, Processing & Nutrition, Distribution, and Consumption & Waste.
      - **Motion:** five areas: Biomimicry, Migration, Water Flow, Driving, and Assisted Mobility.
      - Each area has starting questions, a Massachusetts "Did you know?" fact, testable "Try it" project ideas, and resource links I checked.
      - Each teacher guide flags safety for its topic, such as projects that test with people or involve live animals.
      - MSEF is now testing both modules with teachers across grade levels.
