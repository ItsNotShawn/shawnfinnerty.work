    summary: "MSEF had two module drafts, Food and Motion, and needed them ready for teachers to test. Before designing anything, I worked out what a module should be and how students and teachers move through one."
    details: |
      - **Compared formats.** Google Slides, an interactive Canva deck, Google Forms, a Notion-style workspace, a custom web app and a hybrid, weighed on cost, ease for teachers and how interactive each could be.
      - **Recommended a phased plan.** Start with a framework, worksheets and teacher resources that can be tested quickly, and save a more interactive "project builder" for once the framework proves itself.
      - **Wrote the framework.** A five-step student journey (big idea, explore, generate ideas, narrow down, plan a project), a matching teacher journey, and the parts every module needs.
      - **Revised it with Rebekah at MSEF.** Built around a "big idea" rather than a single challenge, with students picking their own path through four or five areas and ending on a draft research plan.
