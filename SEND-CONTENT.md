# How to send me things for the site

Start a chat with Claude, say **"update my site"** (mention shawnfinnerty.work), then send what you have. Rough notes are fine: I'll write it up, show you a draft before anything goes live, then publish it and check it on the live site.

If you only have a minute, send this much and I'll ask for the rest:

> **Site update:** new project / update [project name] / client work / something else
> **What it is:** one or two sentences, voice memo style is fine
> **Files:** attached, or tell me which folder they're in

## Other things you can send

- **Text changes anywhere:** say the page and paste the new wording (or describe the change).
- **A channel animation:** say which channel (Games, Hardware & Product, Motion & 3D, Design, Photo & Sound, AI) and whether it should loop or move as you scroll.
- **About me / bio, services descriptions, the AI page:** send the new text, even rough.
- **Look and feel changes:** a screenshot with a note ("this feels too dark", "make this bigger") works best.

## Files: what works best

| Type | Best format | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| Cover image | JPG or PNG | Wide (about 16:10), at least 1600px wide. The most important image per project. |
| Process photos | JPG or PNG | Phone photos are fine. Add a few words about each one. |
| Screens / mockups | PNG | Export at 2x if you can. |
| Video | YouTube (unlisted is fine) or Vimeo link | Big video files slow the site down, so a link is best. Short loops under ~10 seconds can be sent as .mp4. |
| Audio / music | SoundCloud link | Private links work too. |
| Channel animation | .mp4 (or .gif) | 16:9, 3 to 8 seconds, under ~2 MB, no sound. |

**How to get files to me:** attach them in the chat (easiest), or put them in a folder on your computer and tell me which one if your computer is linked to the chat. Name them however you like; I'll rename and resize them.

## What I'll do every time

1. Turn your notes into page text in your voice (plain language, no buzzwords) and show you the draft.
2. Resize and add the images, embed the video and audio.
3. Publish once you say it looks good, then check it on the live site (desktop and phone) and tell you what changed.
4. Point out anything that's still a placeholder.
